Step 06

Upload three files to complete AI readiness

After listing, download your personalised AI file pack. It contains three files that make your site directly readable by AI agents — not just listed, but navigable. Your score jumps to 100 once they're in place.

  • llms.txt — AI's entry point to your site. Upload it to your root folder so it lives at yoursite.com/llms.txt
  • ai/index.md — your site in plain AI-readable format. Goes inside an ai/ folder at yoursite.com/ai/index.md
  • ai/sitemap.md — your content map for AI agents. Also in the ai/ folder, at yoursite.com/ai/sitemap.md
