What's actually missing

Every other solution gives artificial intelligence more content. None of them give AI intent.

llms.txt gives AI a readable file. Schema.org gives AI structured data. Sitemaps give AI navigation. These are all answers to the same question: how do I make my content easier to process?

Every web standard tells AI something about your content. Your introduction tells AI why that content exists.

Content tells AI what you publish. Context tells AI what you're about. Your introduction tells AI who you are before any of that begins. Until now, no website had a way to do that.
